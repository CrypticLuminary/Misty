# Privacy Principles

Misty handles intimate group media. Collect and expose only what is needed.

- Originals remain private and untouched.
- Generated previews should remove unnecessary sensitive EXIF.
- GPS-derived experiences are off/hidden by default until the product defines explicit controls.
- Face embeddings are scoped to a Space by default and are not silently reused as a global identity profile.
- Find Me results are probabilistic ("likely matches") and support correction.
- Derived AI artifacts are deleted/rebuilt according to Space retention/deletion policy.
- Telemetry must not contain raw private media, signed URLs, auth credentials or unnecessary sensitive metadata.
- Retention language must match actual storage behavior.

A formal privacy/legal review is required before public production launch; this engineering document is not a substitute for jurisdiction-specific legal advice.
